## Emoji

Emoji (for example the fruit and the memory card pictures in Games) are plain Unicode text.
They are drawn by each device's own emoji font (Apple, Google, Microsoft, Samsung). We do not ship any emoji images.
Do not use screenshots of emoji in ads or videos without checking the emoji maker's terms; prefer our own drawings there.
